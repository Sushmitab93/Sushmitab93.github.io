Experience

  2. 2021 to 2025

    Lead Specialist (CAT Systems Analyst), Gallagher

    Reinsurance catastrophe modeling. Part of the team that built Gaia, an internal platform automating data preparation, import, RMS-to-AIR conversion and model runs, cutting manual processing by 35%. I tested cross-platform conversions, mapped modifier and reinsurance structure tables, wrote validation rules, and optimized SQL Server scripts for reporting, improving efficiency by 8 to 10%.

Helped cut account turnaround from 6 days to 2, built Power BI dashboards for team performance, and trained 6+ analysts.
